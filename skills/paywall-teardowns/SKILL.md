## The 8 moves the winners share (cross-app synthesis)
1. **Value before the ask** — deliver a felt result or investment before the paywall (Headspace's in-onboarding breathing "aha," Duolingo's first lesson before signup). Value-moment-first apps see ~2.1× higher trial starts.
2. **Sunk-cost ramp via long personalized onboarding** — Flo ~70 screens, Noom up to 113. Length isn't a bug: every answer raises the cost of leaving and makes the plan feel bespoke.
3. **Price re-anchoring on the annual plan** — strikethrough, per-month/week breakdown, longer trial on annual (Headspace 14 vs 7 days) to steer to the higher-LTV yearly.
4. **Relentless live A/B testing** — Cal AI ran 123 experiments / 424 variants in 10 months; Headspace price-tests every ~6 months. No one treats the paywall as static.
5. **Loss-aversion beats benefit-listing** — Blinkist's biggest win came from a paywall that listed *zero* features and instead killed the "I'll forget to cancel" fear (+23% signups, −55% complaints).
6. **Curiosity gaps + contextual gating** — Tinder blurs who-liked-you; Duolingo tailors the paywall benefit to the exact friction hit; ChatGPT gates the newest models. Sell the specific thing wanted at the moment it's wanted.
7. **Post-purchase & win-back upsells** — Flo's immediate 33%-off "gift" after subscribing; Cal AI's family-plan/lifetime upsells + lapsed-user win-back. The funnel doesn't end at first purchase.
8. **Simplicity where the decision is hard** — Calm's single plan, ChatGPT's flat $20 kill comparison paralysis (opposite lever from long-quiz apps, same goal).

## Patterns across the 100 (from the census)
- **Paywall type mix:** ~45% hard/hard-onboarding (rising — AI/quiz-health/reading), ~35% freemium (incumbents w/ network effects), ~20% hybrid (dating, TikTok, manga, Audible — fastest growing).
- **Trial:** 7-day is modal, but ~90% of trial starts are Day 0 — the onboarding funnel matters more than trial length.
- **Pricing anchor:** annual shown as monthly-equivalent is near-universal (+28–34% annual mix). Weekly ($2.99–$6.99/wk) is the aggressive-UA anchor for quiz-wall apps.
- **Market context:** subscription revenue hit **$79.5B in 2025 (73% iOS)**; AI is the new top category (ChatGPT/CapCut/WeTV each crossed $1B). Median sub app makes ~$8.3K/mo after 18 months; top 5% exceed $1.16M/mo.

## Cautionary note (compliance)
Cal AI was pulled from the App Store in April 2026 for showing the weekly-equivalent more prominently than the billed amount — a live example of a top performer crossing the line. See `paywall-compliance-guardrails` before copying any weekly-framing or trial-toggle tactic.
