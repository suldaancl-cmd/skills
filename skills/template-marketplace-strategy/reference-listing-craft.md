# Listing Craft — Naming, Thumbnails, Description, Social Proof

## Naming

**Webflow rules (enforced at submission):** 1–2 words, preferably 1, unique, theme-relevant (Orbit for a space-tech template, Haven for wellness), no keyword stuffing, no brand/author name in the title, no slang or odd capitalization. The entire Webflow featured wall follows this uniformly: Vaboulus, Orionix, Aureon, Scalaura, Zelect.

**Framer convention (not a hard rule, but the pattern bestsellers follow):** "Coined Name · Niche Template" — e.g. "Fluence · SaaS & AI Agent Template." The name is the brand; the subtitle is where every marketplace-searchable keyword goes.

**Naming source:** webflow.com/templates (featured wall pattern), framer.com/community/marketplace/templates/fluence/.

## Thumbnails

**Webflow requirement (submission-guideline level, not optional):** the main marketplace-card image must show the homepage cleanly — no angled or tiled mockups. The second thumbnail is the **hover state** and must show a genuinely different section or page in the same visual tone. No badges, no tool icons, no category text, no CTAs baked into either image.

**Why it matters:** the marketplace card is the entire top of funnel. Buyers scan hundreds of same-size cards; the hover-swap is often the only "demo" a browser sees before clicking through. Within the format constraint, winners differentiate through bold type and strong color that reads at thumbnail scale (Origin Studio, Helios Solar — both $129, both featured).

**Framer difference:** marketplace cards autoplay a live scrolling preview rather than a static hover-swap. Invest specifically in the first two viewport-heights of the homepage with an immediate scroll-animation payoff, since that's what plays automatically in the grid.

**Source:** webflow.com/templates/submission-guidelines; webflow.com/templates (featured examples).

## Description copy

1. **First line repeats the niche and buyer types** — "Perfect for creative studios, agencies, SaaS teams, freelancers, and startups." This text is indexed by marketplace search, so front-load the keywords a buyer would actually type.
2. **Name every animation explicitly.** Fullstack Studio's ($99, Webflow) listing spells out "Arc Image Marquee Hero, scroll-triggered text highlights, parallax hero, infinite marquees, scroll-driven stack reveals, animated counters, micro-interactions everywhere." A buyer can't feel your interactions from a screenshot — tell them what's there.
3. **Sold-count proof, once true.** Neither marketplace publishes a public sales counter (Framer shows likes/remixes only; Webflow shows neither). Fluence AI's listing states "500+ copies sold" directly in the copy — this is the seller injecting the only hard proof buyers can't otherwise see. Do not write this line until the number is real.
4. **"Perfect for" niche bullet list** doubles as SEO for marketplace search and as a buyer self-identification cue.
5. **Figma file included, if true.** Both Fullstack Studio (Webflow) and Fluence AI (Framer) headline "🎨 Figma File Included" — it costs nothing if the design source already exists in Figma, and it raises perceived value past the price point for agency buyers who pitch clients in Figma first.

**Sources:** framer.com/community/marketplace/templates/fluence/; webflow.com/templates/html/fullstack-studio-website-template.

## Social proof beyond the description

- **Framer remix/like counts render publicly on every category card** — top free templates show 300–612 remixes (ERIC COLE 612, Hanzo 486, Das Studio 473, Porto 306). This is ambient social proof you don't have to write yourself; drive it up early by launching on X / the Framer community.
- **Webflow's comment tab doubles as testimonials** — ask early buyers to comment; there's no star-rating system, so comments are the only on-page social proof Webflow offers natively.
- **Made-in-Webflow cloneables as a free-distribution funnel:** publish effect-sized (not full-site) free cloneables extracted from your paid templates — one loader, one nav pattern, one text reveal — named by technique so they surface in showcase search. Timothy Ricks' GSAP ScrollTrigger tutorial cloneable has 2.4k clones / 11.2k views; Osmo's Interactions Demo has 1.1k clones. Put your template-store link in the project description and a branded end-frame in the cloneable itself. Teaching-format cloneables (tutorial + editable file) outperform finished-site cloneables roughly 10x in clone count per the research.
- **Framer's free-template funnel is a direct revenue mechanic, not just exposure:** every remix link carries your referral, and Framer pays up to 50% of a subsequent subscription for 12 months (templates only, self-serve subscriptions only). Webflow has no equivalent — free Webflow templates require community support and pay nothing recurring, so on Webflow, free templates are purely for exposure and to pass the designer application.

**Sources:** webflow.com/made-in-webflow/popular; webflow.com/made-in-webflow/gsap; framer.com/help/articles/how-the-creator-program-works/.
