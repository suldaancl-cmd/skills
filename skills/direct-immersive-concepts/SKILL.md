# Direct Immersive Concepts

Design the idea that makes the site inevitable. Choose tools only after the concept survives critique.

## Inputs

Collect or infer:

- audience, offer, desired action, and business constraint
- brand truth that competitors cannot honestly claim
- subject behavior: what the product, material, person, or story physically does
- emotional start and emotional destination
- required content, CMS needs, devices, timeline, and performance ceiling
- references and the specific mechanism admired in each

If references are central, invoke `mine-award-site-patterns` first.

## Create three concept routes

For each route define:

1. **Concept sentence:** “The site behaves like ___ because the brand ___.”
2. **Persistent metaphor:** one object, field, rule, or transformation that can evolve across the entire experience.
3. **User verb:** explore, assemble, reveal, tune, orbit, descend, conduct, grow, distort, or another brand-specific action.
4. **Emotional arc:** arrival → curiosity → participation → understanding → conversion → afterimage.
5. **Signature moment:** one interaction that could be recognized without the logo.
6. **Supporting system:** typography, color/light, scroll pacing, media, sound, navigation, and micro-interactions derived from the same idea.
7. **Quiet mode:** define how ordinary reading, navigation, forms, and mobile work without spectacle.

Never propose a concept that is only an aesthetic label such as “dark luxury,” “futuristic,” or “minimal.”

## Score before selecting

Read `references/concept-scorecard.md`. Score each route from 1–5 on:

- brand inevitability
- originality relative to references and category norms
- emotional clarity
- content usefulness
- interaction potential
- visual-system potential
- technical feasibility
- mobile and reduced-motion translation
- performance risk
- conversion clarity

Reject any route below 35/50 or below 4/5 for brand inevitability. Select one route and preserve the second-best as a fallback.

## Translate concept into a system

Define:

- **hero mechanism:** one primary visual/interaction engine
- **two motion motifs:** repeatable behaviors used across sections
- **type behavior:** what type does that only makes sense for this concept
- **palette/light arc:** how atmosphere changes with the emotional arc
- **spatial model:** flat editorial, layered 2.5D, continuous 3D, or hybrid
- **content grammar:** how case studies, products, or chapters inherit the system
- **sonic role:** none, optional ambience, interaction cues, or audio-reactive world
- **conversion release:** where motion becomes quiet and the choice becomes obvious

Use one hero mechanism. Additional effects must either reinforce the metaphor or be removed.

## Gate before production

- Confirm the concept works as a sentence without naming a technology.
- Confirm typography, color, motion, and interaction all derive from it.
- Confirm the site remains understandable with animation disabled.
- Confirm the signature moment is original enough to avoid imitating a reference.
- Do not begin detailed motion or 3D production until the concept direction is selected.
