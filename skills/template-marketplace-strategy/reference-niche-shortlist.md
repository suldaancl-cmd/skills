## The AI/SaaS exception

AI/SaaS "agent-ready" startup templates sit inside the saturated Technology category (5,965 on Webflow) yet are rated the single top sellability pattern in the research (10/10). Named examples: **Fluence AI** ($49, Framer, "500+ copies sold" — framer.com/community/marketplace/templates/fluence/), **AgentLab** ($129, Framer), **Fusion AI** ($79, Framer). Reasoning from the research: funded AI startups are the buyer pool with the most money and the least time to build their own site, and the keyword "AI" rides marketplace search independent of category crowding. Treat this as the one deliberate exception to "pick a thin category."

## Local-service trade niche (named examples, real pattern)

Roofing, solar, HVAC, cleaning — conversion-focused templates (quote form hero, services grid, before/after gallery, reviews strip, service-area map). Named examples: **Roofivo** (Framer, $39, trending), **Helios Solar** (Webflow, $129, featured). Rationale: millions of local businesses, agency buyers churn these out weekly (repeat purchasers), and Webflow's Home Services category (631) is far thinner than Portfolio (7,045) for a comparably low design bar.
