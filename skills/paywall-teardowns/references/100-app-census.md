# The Paywall-100: A Census of Successful Subscription-Paywall Mobile Apps

**Compiled:** 2026-07-20 · **Method:** WebSearch + WebFetch across Business of Apps, RevenueCat, Sensor Tower, Appfigures, Statista, Superwall, Adapty, screensdesign, and category teardowns.

## VERIFICATION LOCK — how to read this document

Every row carries a **confidence tag** on its revenue/ranking claim:

- **[verified]** — the app's revenue or category-ranking is backed by a cited source in the References section (Business of Apps, Sensor Tower, Statista, company filings, credible press, or a named teardown).
- **[teardown]** — the paywall mechanics (trial/pricing/tactic) come from a named paywall-teardown source (Superwall, screensdesign, Adapty, RevenueCat, Relaunch.ai, Unstar).
- **[unverified]** — the app is a well-known subscription app included by category, but I could **not** attach an individually-cited revenue figure. Its subscription model and approximate public pricing are widely reported, but treat the specific numbers as **unverified**.

Pricing marked **"approx"** is the publicly-listed / widely-reported tier, not a figure I independently sourced per app. Prices are USD unless noted and drift constantly (apps A/B-test pricing per cohort/geo).

Paywall-type definitions used below:
- **Hard** = no core value without paying (or a hard-gate paywall on first open, no free tier).
- **Soft** = paywall shown but dismissible; a usable free tier exists behind it.
- **Freemium** = genuinely usable free product, upgrade optional.
- **Hybrid** = subscription + consumables/à-la-carte, or reverse-trial mechanics.

## PATTERNS ACROSS THE 100

Sourced from RevenueCat State of Subscription Apps 2025 [S2], Adapty 2026 benchmark [S4], and the per-app observations above.

### Paywall type distribution (of the ~104 apps catalogued)
- **Hard / hard-onboarding paywalls:** ~45% and rising — heavily concentrated in AI, quiz-driven health, and reading apps. RevenueCat: hard paywalls convert install→paid at a **12.1% median vs 2.2% for freemium** [S2], which is why new AI/health entrants default to hard.
- **Freemium:** ~35% — dominant among incumbents with network effects or ad revenue (music, big AI chatbots, Duolingo, MyFitnessPal).
- **Hybrid (subscription + consumables / reverse-trial):** ~20% — dating (Tinder/Bumble/Hinge/Grindr), TikTok, manga/webtoon coin economies, Audible credits. This is the fastest-growing structure; RevenueCat's 2025 headline was "the shift away from pure subscriptions" [S2b].

### Dominant trial length
- **7-day free trial is the modal choice** across the census. RevenueCat notes **82% of trial starts happen the same day as install** and Adapty puts **~90% of trial starts on Day 0** [S2][S4] — so trial *length* matters less than the onboarding funnel that precedes it.
- Counter-signal: RevenueCat finds **longer 17–32-day trials convert highest at 45.7%** [S2], but few apps use them because early monetization/UA-payback pressure favors 3–7 days. Duolingo's 14-day *reverse* trial is the notable outlier.

### Dominant pricing anchor
- **Annual plan displayed as a monthly-equivalent** is the near-universal anchor ("$4.99/mo billed annually" vs "$59.99/yr"). Adapty: framing the annual price monthly **lifts annual selection 28–34%** [S4].
- Category annual medians (RevenueCat) [S2]: **Health & Fitness ≈ $29.65/yr (3.8× monthly $7.73); Education ≈ $43.94/yr (5.4× monthly $8.13)**. AI apps anchor higher ($20/mo ChatGPT/Perplexity/Claude tier).
- **Weekly pricing** ($2.99–$6.99/wk) is the aggressive-UA anchor for quiz-wall apps (Cal AI, Remini, ELSA, Headway) — and is exactly what got **Cal AI pulled from the App Store in April 2026** for showing the weekly-equivalent more prominently than the billed amount [S25].

### The 10 tactics that repeat most
1. **Quiz/onboarding personalization before the paywall** — "your custom plan is ready." Cal AI, Noom, BetterMe, ELSA, Blinkist, Headway. RevenueCat + Adapty both name a brief onboarding quiz as a top lever [S2][S4].
2. **Value-moment before the wall** — let the user feel the product first. Apps that trigger the paywall after a value moment see **2.1× higher trial-start rates** [S4]. (Fitbod free workouts, Photomath free answer, Brilliant free lesson.)
3. **Annual-as-monthly price framing** — +28–34% annual mix [S4]; effectively every app in the census.
4. **Loss-aversion / streak mechanics** — Duolingo's streak + hearts is the archetype; drives daily return and contextual paywalls [S13][S14].
5. **Reverse trial** (full premium first, then downgrade) — Duolingo's 14-day Super; converts by making the free tier feel worse *after* [S13].
6. **Post-decline downsell / 24-hour welcome offer** — one-time discount to non-converters. Cal AI's $120→$20/yr downsell [S23]; Adapty recommends a 24h non-converter offer [S4].
7. **Consumables layered on subscription** — Super Likes/Roses/Boosts (dating), Coins/gifts (TikTok, StarMaker, manga). Extracts more from whales than a flat sub.
8. **Context-aware paywall copy** — same app, different pitch by entry point. Duolingo shows "unlimited hearts" from the shop vs "remove ads" from an ad [S13].
9. **Free-tier friction as the seller** — algorithmic deprioritization (Tinder), shuffle-only/ads (Spotify), page/scan caps (CamScanner, Drops 5-min cap). The free tier is engineered to frustrate.
10. **Trial-reminder / SSO-streamlined signup** — reducing registration friction with SSO and same-day trial capture; RevenueCat lists SSO streamlining as a core paywall lever [S2].

### Cross-cutting market facts (all [verified])
- Subscription revenue hit **$79.5B in 2025, iOS = 73%** [S1].
- **AI is the new top category:** ChatGPT, CapCut, and WeTV each crossed **$1B IAP** in 2025; AI apps generated $18.5B (ChatGPT = 43%) [S3][S22].
- **Winner-take-most:** RevenueCat — median sub app makes **$8.3K/mo after 18 months; top 5% exceed $1.16M/mo** [S2]. The census's named apps are almost all in that top sliver.

## Verification summary

- **~40 apps** carry a directly-cited revenue or category-ranking source ([verified]) — the category leaders that appeared explicitly in Business of Apps, Sensor Tower, Statista, Global Dating Insights, Superwall, Appfigures, or company filings.
- **~14 apps** have paywall mechanics from a named teardown ([teardown]: Superwall, screensdesign, Adapty, RevenueCat, Relaunch.ai, Unstar, nutriscan/eesel).
- **~50 apps** are included by category as well-known subscription apps with widely-reported public pricing but **no individually-cited revenue figure** — flagged **[unverified]** on the revenue dimension. Their subscription model and approximate pricing tiers are public knowledge; the specific numbers were not independently sourced per app.
- **All prices are "approx"** — apps A/B-test and localize pricing continuously; treat every dollar figure as an indicative tier, not a locked quote.
