# The ayzz.thedesigner Design-Reel Formula

Abdul Mutakabar Ayaz (**@ayzz.thedesigner**, ~264K followers) built a large design audience and an agency pipeline (**@zz.studio.design**) by packaging real design work into short, punchy, value-dense reels. This skill is the reusable formula behind them — for scripting and producing design content in his style.

His whole engine: **give away the real process for free → grow a design audience → convert attention into followers, saved plugins/resources, and "design my site" DMs.** The content is the top of a funnel, not the product.

## The reel skeleton (every video)

**1. Hook (first 1–2 seconds) — pattern interrupt or promise.** This is 80% of performance. He uses three hook types:

- **Contrarian / myth-bust** — say the thing the audience half-believes, then flip it:
  - *"Your websites are too animated."*
  - *"AI can design better than you."*
  - *"You need multiple AI tools for your design workflow."* (→ then: no, you don't)
- **Curiosity / promise** — tease an outcome + a surprise:
  - *"If you want to build websites like this — the answer may honestly surprise you."*
- **Value list** — name the deliverable up front:
  - *"Tall fonts I'd use in every design."* / *"Fav Free Fonts, Part 5."*

**2. Retention promise (right after the hook).** Tell them why to stay: *"If you make it to the end, I'll share the exact process and tools we use."* Sets an open loop that closes only at the end.

**3. Body — pick one of three formats** (see below).

**4. Payoff / reveal.** Close the loop with a satisfying result: the finished cinematic site, the transformation, the punchline. His signature reveal line: *"All these assets from just one picture."*

**5. CTA — engineered for the algorithm AND leads** (see CTA section).

## The 3 reel formats

### A. Build-along (screen recording)
Screen-record the Figma build, sped up, with burned-in captions narrating each step: brief on screen → **Colors & Fonts** → generate imagery (Higgsfield) → reveal the finished site. The arc is a **transformation** (one product photo → full cinematic brand site). Most shareable format. Use it whenever you have a real build to show.

### B. Talking-head explainer
Face to camera in a consistent setup (see production), explaining a concept or his process, with burned-in captions so it works on mute. Best for teaching the "why" — process, philosophy, tool stack. He ends these by closing the loop on the retention promise (reveals the tools) then asks for the follow.

### C. Resource / font showcase
A fast visual reel — his profile photo or a clean frame + **big type specimens / swatches** cycling on color-blocked backgrounds, each labeled (font name, hex). Low-effort, high-save. Runs as a **named series** ("Fav Free Fonts, Part N") so it compounds follows and saves over time.

## The CTA — comment-to-DM is the engine

His highest-leverage move: a **comment-to-DM gate**.

> *"Comment 'Higgs' and I'll send the link of the plugin in your DM."*

Why it works: it manufactures a flood of comments (the single strongest ranking signal), and every comment becomes a **DM thread** — a warm contact he can pitch resources or agency work to. Always pick a short, on-topic keyword tied to the payoff (the tool, the file, the template).

Secondary CTA, for talking-head/teaching reels: a soft **follow nudge** that rewards curiosity — *"If you want to learn more about this stuff, why aren't you following me?"*

Rule: **one CTA per reel.** Comment-to-DM when you have an asset to give; follow-nudge when you're teaching.

## Caption structure

He writes captions as a mini story + positioning + CTA + niche tags. Template:

```
[Comment-keyword CTA — first line, so it's visible unexpanded]

[The project story — start at the brief]
"This whole project started with one product image and a brief that said: make it
feel like the candle itself. Warm, atmospheric, quiet luxury."

[What you actually did / the unlock]
"Everything you see — hero, product shots, promo video — generated inside Figma
using Higgsfield. No photoshoot. No switching tabs. No paying for five tools."

[Positioning line — the identity sell]
"This is how designers stay ahead."

[Niche hashtags]
#uiux #figma #webdesign #higgsfield #uidesign
```

Notes:
- **Lead the caption with the comment keyword** so the CTA shows before "…more".
- **Start the story at the brief** — it pulls people who do client work.
- **End with an identity line** ("this is how designers stay ahead") — sells belonging, not just a tip.
- **Niche, not broad, hashtags** — design-specific (#uiux #figma #webdesign), plus the tool name when relevant.

## Production signatures (the recognizable look)
- **Vertical 1080×1920**, designed to be watched on mute (burned-in captions always).
- **His face** appears (talking head or profile cutout) — builds familiarity and trust.
- **Consistent set**: same room (wood panelling), gaming chair, RGB keyboard, plant. Recognizable frame = personal brand.
- **Screen recordings as proof** — show the real Figma canvas, not slides.
- **Fast pace**, captions chunked to a few words at a time.
- **Teach real tools** by name (Figma, Higgsfield, Webflow, GSAP, Lenis, Barba.js) — specificity = credibility.

## Content strategy that makes it compound
- **Series** ("Fav Free Fonts Part N", project build series) — recurring formats train the audience to expect and save.
- **One reel = one idea** with one CTA.
- **Mine your real work**: every client build is 3+ reels (the build-along, a tool tip, a before/after, a font/color breakdown).
- **Hook is contrarian or promise** — never a boring "Here's a tutorial."
- **Free value up top, funnel underneath** — the plugin link / resource is the bait; the DM is the catch; the agency is the close.

## How to script a reel with this skill
1. Pick the **idea** (a build, a tool, a font set, a myth to bust).
2. Write **3 hook options** (one contrarian, one promise, one value-list) and pick the punchiest.
3. Add the **retention promise**.
4. Choose the **format** (A build-along / B talking-head / C showcase).
5. Script the **body** as captioned beats; land the **payoff/reveal**.
6. Write **one CTA** (comment-to-DM if there's an asset, else follow-nudge).
7. Draft the **caption** with the template above.
