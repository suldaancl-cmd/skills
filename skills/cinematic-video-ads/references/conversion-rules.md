# Lead-Gen Conversion Rules (short-form)

The data layer. What actually makes a short cinematic ad generate leads (DMs, WhatsApp, bookings, clicks). Every claim is sourced; treat numbers as directional benchmarks, not guarantees.

## Conversion Rules

1. **Win or lose in the first 0.5–3s.** Hook rate (3-sec views ÷ impressions) is the first filter. Meta Reels median ≈ 20%; 25–30% solid; 30–40% good; 40%+ elite. Reels runs 5–10 pts below Feed (faster scroll). Below 15% = the ad loses the auction before the offer is seen. [MHI Growth Engine; Coinis; zeely.ai; Benly.ai]

2. **~73% of ecommerce video ads fail because they look like ads.** Pattern interruption in frame 1 is the entry requirement. Scroll speed +41% since 2020; viewers decide in <0.5s. Best openers: unexpected visual reveals, bold contrarian statements, direct pain-point callouts, "wait for it" setups, before/after previews. [motionapp.com; Predictive Marketing]

3. **Ideal length: 15–35s for pure lead-gen hooks; 25–45s when an offer needs explaining.** 15–30s videos hit up to 75% completion if the hook pays off within 5s. TikTok 21–34s = highest completion (62%). Over 60s drops to 48%. [Benly.ai; OpusClip; Shortimize]

4. **Retention follows the open-loop, not the sales funnel.** Structure: Hook (0–3s) → Problem Escalation (3s→80% of runtime) → Payoff (last 15–25%) → CTA (last 3–5s). Pose a question and withhold the answer; never give the punchline in the first 5s. Viral Shorts (1M+) avg ~76% retention; >75% retention = 3× higher algorithmic push. [virvid.ai; opus.pro; Shortimize]

5. **85% of social video is watched muted — design silent-first (Meta).** Captions are the primary storytelling layer, not a transcript. Use large branded fonts (not default subtitles), kinetic/color-coded key phrases, reposition text through the frame, and test on mute before publishing. [clicks.video; Manchester Digital; Meta data]

6. **Captions lift engagement up to 40%** — when they act as narrative (punchlines, transitions, curiosity builders "What happens next is wild", step indicators "Step 1 of 3"), not transcription. Dual-coding (audio + on-screen text) beats either alone. [clicks.video; getkoro.app]

7. **Native, creator-style content beats polished commercial by 25–40% on Meta Reels.** Smartphone feel, conversational delivery, trending audio win. TikTok Creative Codes: "TikTok doesn't reward polished; it rewards real." Fails: slow builds, stock footage, heavy logo intros, branded supers in the first 3s. [Benly.ai Q1 2026; TikTok Creative Codes]

8. **Reels beats Feed: 20–35% lower CPM, ~2× engagement, +13% incremental conversions.** Benly.ai: Reels CPM $6.20 vs Feed $9.40; CTR 1.8% vs 0.9%. Meta: Reels-first ads with music/VO drive +13% incremental conversions, up to −16% CPA. [Benly.ai; socialmediatoday/Meta]

9. **On TikTok, sound is a conversion driver — design sound-ON.** Opposite of Meta's silent-first rule: TikTok ads with sound lift purchase intent + brand favorability (official Creative Codes). Music, VO, SFX carry emotional load; budget for trending audio or original VO. [TikTok Creative Codes]

10. **One CTA, stated twice (spoken + on-screen), at the end.** Single-CTA pages convert 13.5% vs 10.5% for 3+ CTAs (Unbounce, 18,639 pages). In short-form: one action, last 3–5s, said aloud AND shown as text. Spoken+visual dual-coding beats visual-only CTAs by ~35% ROAS. [getkoro.app; Unbounce via wisernotify]

11. **WhatsApp/DM CTAs crush link-click CTAs for conversational lead-gen.** WhatsApp Business API campaigns avg 45–60% CTR on the message link vs 2–5% for email/SMS. Click-to-WhatsApp and TikTok DM/instant-form (can route to WhatsApp) lower friction because messaging is native. [sendwo 2025; infobip; sleekflow; TikTok docs]

12. **Creative fatigue hits ~day 7 — rotate hooks, not whole ads.** A given hook drops ~37% after 7 days. When hook rate falls 20%+ WoW, swap the opener; keep body + CTA. Run 3–5 hook variants at once; decide at the 4-week mark. [motionapp.com; Predictive Marketing]

## Hook Templates (fill the `[ ]`)
1. **Agitated problem (PAS):** "Still [pain]? You might be making this one [mistake] every [day/week]."
2. **Status-quo flip:** "Stop trying to fix your [symptom]. The real problem is your [root cause]."
3. **Bold claim + proof tease:** "We got [result] in [timeframe] without [thing they hate]. Here's the breakdown."
4. **Founder's confession:** "I [burned] [metric] on [wrong approach]. Here's the one thing that worked."
5. **Audience callout:** "[Identity, e.g. property investors in Riyadh]: if [problem], watch this."
6. **Curiosity gap:** "Don't watch this unless you're serious about [outcome]."
7. **Contrarian:** "Why your [popular solution] is making [problem] worse — and what to do instead."
8. **Number hook:** "[X]% of [audience] who [tried approach] still fail because of [overlooked factor]."
9. **Transformation tease:** "[Time] ago I was [negative state]. This is what changed."
10. **Pain-mirror question:** "Are you [negative feeling] every time you try to [outcome]?"

## CTA Templates (ONE per ad)
1. **Comment-to-DM:** "Comment '[keyword]' and I'll DM you the [price list/details] now."
2. **Click-to-WhatsApp:** "Tap to message me on WhatsApp — I reply personally."
3. **Booking + scarcity:** "We take only [X] new clients/month. Link in bio for the last [slots]."
4. **Lead magnet:** "Type '[word]' in comments and I'll send the free [checklist/guide]."
5. **Link-in-bio:** "Click the link in my bio — I'll show you how to [outcome] in [timeframe]."
6. **Social proof + DM:** "Join the [number] who already [result]. DM '[word]' to start."
7. **Consult call:** "If [problem] sounds familiar, book a free [15-min] call — link below."
8. **Comment-gating:** "Comment '[X]' and I'll reply with [thing they want] — I check every one."
