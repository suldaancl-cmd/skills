## Free tools/sites these creators surface
From bob_graphix's "Tools I can't work without, Part 2" (creator: Mohab Mohy):
- **mockups-design.com** — free high-quality mockups (paper bag, can, signage, brochure, apparel) to present designs realistically.
- **colorhunt.co** — curated color-palette inspiration; searchable, save-friendly.
- **unblast.com** — free PSDs, mockups, and fonts in one place.

Fonts seen in these carousels (display/editorial taste, overlaps with the ayzz font reference): Magnolia, Vinetra, Ruiga, Borscha, Sillage, almarena monospaced.

## Worked palette example (dyslove.design, "New Color Combos")
Two curated pairs: **Carbon + Lime** and **Onyx + Candy Blue**. Each color shown on its own slide as a glass card over a matching cinematic photo, with full values. Captured value:

| Color | CMYK | RGB | HEX |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Carbon** | 0, 0, 0, 91 | 23, 23, 23 | **#171717** |

(Lime / Onyx / Candy Blue follow the same card treatment on subsequent slides.) The takeaway isn't this specific hex — color combos are infinite and easily generated with `color-system` / `colorhunt.co` / `ui-ux-pro-max`. The reusable lessons are the **presentation** (named color + full values on a tone-matched cinematic card) and the **content mechanics** above.

## How to use this list
- **Borrow formats, not assets.** Match the slide treatment and CTA mechanics; generate your own palettes/fonts/tool picks.
- **For Karim:** model **bob_graphix** for the Arabic-first MENA angle (tools + resources in Egyptian/Gulf Arabic, tool names in English), and **dyslove.design** for the premium glass-card color treatment. Pair with the comment-to-DM gate to build a design-lead list.
