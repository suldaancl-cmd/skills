## Selection test

- Fits the brand truth, not merely the industry.
- Supports readable long-form content.
- Produces a distinctive thumbnail and a calm conversion area.
- Works in DOM, canvas, video, loading, and fallback states.
- Has a practical font license and asset pipeline.
- Does not depend on a fashionable effect to feel complete.
