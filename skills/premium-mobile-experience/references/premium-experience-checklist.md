# Premium experience checklist

Score only observed behavior: pass, needs work, not applicable, or not tested.

## Input and feedback

- Touch feedback begins immediately.
- Buttons cannot accidentally submit twice.
- Haptics match meaningful state changes.
- Disabled and pending states remain legible.

## Continuity

- Navigation preserves orientation.
- Lists do not jump after refresh or optimistic updates.
- Keyboard and bottom controls coordinate correctly.
- Interrupted animations settle into a valid state.

## Latency and failure

- Layout remains stable while loading.
- Long tasks expose progress and can be resumed.
- Offline and retry behavior is clear.
- Error messages preserve user input and offer a next action.

## Brand craft

- Illustration and motion support the product's identity.
- Empty and success states use product-specific language.
- Sound and celebration are optional, restrained, and accessible.

## Performance and inclusion

- Representative flows meet the project's frame-time target.
- Reduced motion has a coherent fallback.
- Dynamic type and RTL do not break layout.
- Contrast, focus, labels, and touch targets are verified.
