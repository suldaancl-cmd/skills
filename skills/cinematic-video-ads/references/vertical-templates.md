# Ad Structures, Vertical Templates & Benchmarks

Pick a structure by funnel stage + category, then a copyable shot-by-shot template by vertical. Adapt — don't follow blindly.

## Ad Structures (pick one)

**1. AIDA** (Attention→Interest→Desire→Action). Cold audiences, mass-appeal DTC, app installs. 15–30s: Attention (0–3s arresting visual/claim) → Interest (3–8s relatable situation) → Desire (8–20s transformation/benefit-in-use) → Action (20–30s single CTA + friction reducer).

**2. PAS** (Problem→Agitate→Solution). High-pain verticals (health, finance, legal, SaaS). Problem (0–3s name the pain) → Agitate (3–10s cost of inaction) → Solution glimpse (10–18s product as relief) → Proof spike (18–24s one stat) → CTA (24–30s low-commitment).

**3. Three-Act / Story Arc.** Brand-building, founder/origin, high-ticket (real estate, luxury, coaching). 30–60s: Setup (0–6s character + inciting problem) → Confrontation (6–40s struggle + product as turning point) → Resolution (40–55s transformed outcome → CTA).

**4. Before / After Transformation.** Visible-result categories (beauty, fitness, renovation, cleaning). Before (0–3s problem visual) → Transition (3–5s wipe/split/match-cut) → After reveal (5–15s result held + stat overlay) → Social proof (15–22s) → CTA (22–28s).

**5. Founder / Origin Story.** Trust-building for new/premium brands, skeptical categories. Vulnerability hook (0–4s founder names the frustration) → Journey (4–18s raw build montage) → Product reveal (18–28s in founder's hands) → Proof/mission (28–40s) → CTA (40–50s first-buyer offer).

**6. Product Hero / "The Reveal".** Single aesthetic hero product, electronics, luxury, high-visual DTC. Tease/partial reveal (0–3s ECU/blur) → Product in motion (3–10s slow-mo/360) → Feature proof (10–18s one differentiator) → Lifestyle integration (18–24s in use) → CTA (24–28s).

**7. Testimonial-Led.** Mid-funnel retargeting, services, social-proof-led. Result hook (0–3s outcome first) → Skepticism admission (3–8s) → Discovery/process (8–16s) → Transformation (16–22s) → CTA (22–28s). UGC > polished here.

**8. Day-in-the-Life / Lifestyle.** Aspirational brands (fitness, beverage, travel, creator tools). Morning hook (0–3s product appears naturally) → Activity montage (3–15s 3–4 life moments on music beats) → Emotional peak (15–20s) → CTA (20–28s soft ask).

## Benchmark Examples (study these)
- **Dollar Shave Club — "Our Blades Are F***ing Great"** (2012): CEO one-liner hook + comedic 3-act + founder-on-camera. 30M+ views → $1B exit. (YouTube)
- **Squatty Potty — "This Unicorn…"** (Harmon Brothers, 2015): absurdist world sustains 3 min; demo embedded in entertainment. +600% online sales. (YouTube)
- **Purple Mattress — "Raw Egg Test"** (Harmon Brothers, 2016): single proof mechanism does the selling; PAS. (YouTube)
- **Gymshark — UGC mirror-selfie series** (Meta): lifestyle integration; looks organic. (Meta Ad Library → "Gymshark")
- **Poppi — Super Bowl + Alix Earle TikTok** (2024–25): day-in-the-life; soda as identity. (ispot.tv; TikTok)
- **Ridge Wallet — objection-mirror DTC** (Meta 2020–24): customer language verbatim; PAS. (Meta Ad Library, longest-running)
- **Chatbooks — "Mom Chaos"** (Harmon Brothers, 2016): relatable day-in-the-life parody.

## Mistakes to Avoid
- Opening with the logo/brand name (loses ~40% before the hook).
- Slow cinematic build with **no hook payload** — cinematic is a style, not a structure.
- Feature dumping (5+ features kills desire; one core benefit per ad).
- Designing for sound-on on Meta (~70–85% muted).
- Wrong aspect ratio (horizontal on TikTok/Reels signals "repurposed").
- CTA after the drop-off point (80% gone by halfway on 30s ads; plant soft CTA mid, hard CTA end).
- Speaking to everyone (no specificity → broad cheap delivery to non-converters).
- Over-polishing away authenticity (UGC/founder beats studio for trust categories).
- "Storytelling" with no product moment (entertained ≠ converted).
- No friction reduction at CTA ("Try free"/"No commitment" can ~2× CTR).
