# Research Track 2 — Lead-Gen Short-Form Conversion Science
_Source: background researcher · captured 2026-06-02 · all claims source-cited_

## Conversion Rules

**Rule 1 — Win or lose in the first 0.5–3 seconds.**
Hook rate (3-second views / impressions) is the first filter. On Meta Reels, the platform median sits near 20%; 25–30% is solid; 30–40% is good; 40%+ is elite. Reels runs 5–10 points below Feed because scroll velocity is higher — a 25% hook rate on Reels is functionally equivalent to a 35% hook rate in Feed. Below 15% means the ad loses the auction before the offer is visible.
[confirmed — sources: MHI Growth Engine, Coinis, zeely.ai, Benly.ai benchmarks]

**Rule 2 — 73% of ecommerce video ads fail because they look like ads.**
Pattern interruption in the first frame is not a creative preference — it is the entry requirement. Scroll speed has accelerated 41% since 2020; viewers decide in under half a second. The most reliably scroll-stopping openers: unexpected visual reveals, bold contrarian statements, direct pain-point callouts, "wait for it" setups, and before/after previews.
[confirmed — source: motionapp.com, Predictive Marketing]

**Rule 3 — Ideal ad length: 15–35 seconds for pure lead-gen hooks; 25–45 seconds when an offer explanation is needed.**
Well-structured 15–30 second videos achieve up to 75% completion when they deliver on the hook within 5 seconds. For conversion objectives, Benly.ai reports 25–45 seconds as the Meta Reels lead-gen sweet spot. For TikTok, 21–34 seconds achieves the highest average completion rate (62%). Videos over 60 seconds see completion drop to 48%.
[confirmed — sources: Benly.ai, OpusClip/opus.pro, Shortimize]

**Rule 4 — Retention follows the open-loop structure, not the sales funnel.**
The structure that outperforms: Hook (0–3s) → Problem Escalation (3s–80% of runtime) → Payoff (last 15–25%) → CTA (last 3–5s). Posing a question and withholding the answer is the core open-loop mechanic. Never give the punchline in the first 5 seconds. Viral Shorts (1M+ views) average ~76% retention; Shorts above 75% retention have a 3x higher chance of algorithmic push.
[confirmed — sources: virvid.ai, opus.pro, Shortimize]

**Rule 5 — 85% of social video views happen without sound. Design for silence first.**
Facebook/Meta's own data puts muted viewing at 85%. Silent-first design means captions are the primary storytelling layer — not a transcript backup. Key practices: large branded fonts over platform-default subtitles, kinetic/color-coded text to highlight key phrases, repositioning text throughout the frame (not anchored at bottom), and testing the full video on mute before publishing.
[confirmed — sources: clicks.video, Manchester Digital, Meta/Facebook platform data, leadenforce.com]

**Rule 6 — Adding captions lifts engagement by up to 40%.**
Captioning increases engagement when captions function as narrative structure — punchlines, transitions, curiosity builders ("What happens next is wild"), step indicators ("Step 1 of 3") — not mere transcription. Dual-coding (simultaneous audio instruction + on-screen text) outperforms either alone.
[confirmed — sources: clicks.video, getkoro.app, multiple Meta creative guides]

**Rule 7 — Native, creator-style content outperforms polished commercial creative by 25–40% on Meta Reels.**
Smartphone filming, conversational delivery, and trending audio consistently beat produced commercial video. TikTok's own Creative Codes state: "TikTok doesn't reward polished; it rewards real." What fails: slow builds, stock footage, heavy logo intros, branded supers in the first 3 seconds.
[confirmed — sources: Benly.ai Q1 2026 data, TikTok Creative Codes]

**Rule 8 — Reels outperforms Feed: 20–35% lower CPM, 2x higher engagement, 13% higher incremental conversions.**
Benly.ai reports Reels CPM at $6.20 vs Feed at $9.40, CTR at 1.8% vs 0.9%. Meta's own data shows Reels-first ads with music or voiceover drive 13% higher incremental conversions and up to 16% lower CPA.
[confirmed — sources: Benly.ai, socialmediatoday.com/Meta for Business]

**Rule 9 — On TikTok, sound is a conversion driver, not a background element.**
TikTok is singular: ads with sound drive significant lifts in purchase intent and brand favorability (TikTok Creative Codes, official). This is the OPPOSITE of the Meta silent-first rule — TikTok ads should be designed sound-on, with music, voiceover, and SFX carrying emotional load.
[confirmed — source: TikTok Creative Codes]

**Rule 10 — One CTA, stated twice (verbally and visually), placed at the end.**
Single-CTA pages convert at 13.5%; three-or-more-CTA pages drop to 10.5% (Unbounce, 18,639 landing pages). For short-form video: one primary action, in the last 3–5 seconds, stated aloud AND shown as text overlay. Spoken + visual dual-coding outperforms visual-only CTAs by 35% in ROAS.
[confirmed — sources: getkoro.app, Unbounce via wisernotify.com]

**Rule 11 — WhatsApp and DM CTAs dramatically outperform link-click CTAs for conversational lead-gen.**
WhatsApp Business API campaigns average 45–60% CTR on the message link, versus 2–5% for email or SMS. Click-to-WhatsApp and TikTok DM Ads (route to WhatsApp/Messenger/chat) lower friction because messaging is native to the platform. TikTok's instant form can redirect to WhatsApp directly.
[confirmed — sources: sendwo.com 2025, infobip.com, sleekflow.io, TikTok Ads Manager docs]

**Rule 12 — Creative fatigue hits at day 7. Rotate hooks, not entire ads.**
Performance on a given hook drops 37% after 7 days average. When hook rate declines 20%+ WoW, archive that hook variant and rotate a new opener — body and CTA can stay. Test 3–5 hook variants simultaneously; decide at the 4-week mark.
[confirmed — sources: motionapp.com, Predictive Marketing 2025]

## Hook Templates (fill the `[ ]`)

1. **Agitated problem (PAS):** "Still [pain point]? You might be making this one [mistake] every [day/week]."
2. **Status-quo flip:** "Stop trying to fix your [symptom]. The real problem is your [root cause]."
3. **Bold claim + proof tease:** "We got [specific result] in [timeframe] without [thing they hate]. Here's the breakdown."
4. **Founder's confession:** "I [burned] [metric] on [wrong approach]. Here's the one thing that actually worked."
5. **Audience callout:** "[Specific identity, e.g. Wedding photographers in Dubai]: if [problem], watch this [time] video."
6. **Curiosity gap:** "Don't watch this unless you're serious about [outcome]."
7. **Contrarian:** "Why your [popular solution] is making [problem] worse — and what to do instead."
8. **Number hook:** "[X]% of [audience] who [tried approach] still fail because of [overlooked factor]."
9. **Transformation tease:** "[Time] ago I was [negative state]. This is what changed."
10. **Pain-mirror question:** "Are you [negative feeling] every time you try to [outcome]?"

## CTA Templates (one per ad)

1. **Comment-to-DM:** "Comment '[keyword]' and I'll DM you the [price list/details] now."
2. **Click-to-WhatsApp:** "Tap to message me on WhatsApp — I reply personally."
3. **Booking + scarcity:** "We take only [X] new clients/month. Link in bio for the last [slots]."
4. **Lead magnet:** "Type '[word]' in comments and I'll send the free [checklist/guide]."
5. **Link-in-bio:** "Click the link in my bio — I'll show you how to [outcome] in [timeframe]."
6. **Social proof + DM:** "Join the [number] who already [result]. DM '[word]' to start."
7. **Consult call:** "If [problem] sounds familiar, book a free [15-min] call — link below."
8. **Comment-gating:** "Comment '[X]' and I'll reply with [thing they want] — I check every one."
