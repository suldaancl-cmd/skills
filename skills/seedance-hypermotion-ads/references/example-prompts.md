# Hyper-Motion — 5 Ready Category Prompts

Swap the product in and render. All built for 9:16, 1080p, 15s = 3×5s. Start from a locked product still (image-to-video). Paste the negative line every time.

## 1. Perfume / fragrance
```
Luxury glass perfume bottle hyper-motion ad, 9:16, 1080p.
Clip 1 (0–5s): macro close-up of the faceted glass bottle on wet black stone; fast speed-ramp push-in snapping to a hard stop on the cap. Whip-pan entry, motion blur, golden specular glints. Lighting: hard rim light, amber highlights. SFX: airy whoosh + glass chime.
Clip 2 (5–10s): bottle rotates mid-air in a slow 360° orbit while a ribbon of golden liquid and fine mist swirls around it in slow-mo, then speed-ramps back. Camera arcs around the bottle. Lighting: warm amber gel + backlight haze.
Clip 3 (10–15s): hero shot — bottle lands on a polished gold pedestal, quick dolly-out, mist settles, label sharp and centered, clean space above for logo. Lighting: soft studio key.
Style: glossy luxury commercial, high-contrast, warm gold + deep black palette, crisp reflections, shallow DOF. Keep label sharp, readable, unchanged.
Negative: no warping, no melting label, no extra text, no watermark, no distortion.
```
