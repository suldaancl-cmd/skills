# Creator Economics — Commission, Pricing Mechanics, Earnings

All figures below are as captured in the July 2026 research fetch. Re-verify against the live marketplace pages before quoting any number to a client — commission rates in particular (see conflict below).

## The commission-rate conflict

Two passes of the same research produced two different Webflow commission figures:

- **95% to designer / 5% to Webflow** — "commission now 95% to designer (Webflow keeps 5%, cut from 20% in Oct 2025 — banner live on webflow.com/templates/applications)." This is the more specific claim: it names a mechanism (a policy change), a date (Oct 2025), and a location (a live banner on a named URL).
- **Flat 80% commission to sellers** — appears in a separate research pass discussing the price-ladder mechanic, citing the same submission-guidelines-adjacent source plus Bryn Taylor's seller write-up.

These cannot both be current truth for the same policy. Possible explanations the research doesn't resolve: the 80% figure may predate the Oct 2025 change and simply wasn't updated in that section, or the two figures could describe different things (e.g., a promotional rate vs. a standard rate). **Do not present either number as settled fact.** Check `webflow.com/templates/applications` directly before it affects a real pricing or platform-choice decision.

Framer has no equivalent ambiguity in the research: **0% marketplace commission on template sales (100% to creator)**, plus a separate **referral commission of up to 50% of a subscription's value for 12 months** when a buyer who remixed your template later upgrades to a paid Framer plan. This referral commission applies only to templates (not components/plugins/vectors) and only to self-serve subscriptions, per the Framer Creator Program help doc (framer.com/help/articles/how-the-creator-program-works/).

## Webflow's price ladder (feature-checklist pricing)

Webflow does not let sellers set a price — the price is assigned based on which functionality tiers the template includes. Two versions of the ladder appear in the research; they roughly agree and are merged here:

| Price | Trigger |
|---|---|
| $24 | One-page, no CMS |
| $29–$39 | Single-page (general observed range) |
| $34 | One-page + CMS, or multi-page with no CMS |
| $49 | Multi-page + CMS |
| $59–$99 | Multi-page CMS (general observed range) |
| $79 | CMS + Ecommerce, or CMS + UI Kit |
| $99 | Memberships |
| $129 | CMS + Ecommerce + UI Kit (also general "CMS+Ecom+UI-kit+page-variations" band starts here, up to $169) |
| $149–$169 | All of the above + "Multi Layout" — at least 3 layout variations across 3 static pages (e.g., 3 Home variants, 3 About variants, 3 Pricing variants) |

**Worked example from the research:** a Multi-Layout CMS template costs roughly 2x the build time of a $49 template but earns roughly 3x per sale — a direct incentive to feature-stack rather than ship minimal templates. BRIX Templates (the largest studio referenced, 200-300+ templates) sells almost everything at the top $169 tier with a Figma file bundled in.

**Featured/bestseller price clustering (from live marketplace observation):** LeadCraft $169, Helios Solar / ORIGIN Studio / Zelect / Cedar $129 — the featured wall skews $79–$129, meaning buyers demonstrably accept the higher tiers when the template earns it.

**Irreversible landmine:** entering a real business address into a Webflow Ecommerce template project (address, shipping, tax, payment, hosting, checkout setup steps) is irreversible and permanently disqualifies that project from the marketplace the instant any step is completed. Never do this on a template project — only on a real client site.

**Webflow runs its own sitewide 50%-off promos** (visible promo code cached on live listing pages) that it controls, not the seller. Sellers absorb this at their commission rate. This is one argument for also selling via fulfillment links on your own site, where you set — and keep — the full price.

## Framer's price bands (creator-set)

Observed distribution from the research:
- **Free** — rank-farming / referral-funnel tier. Top free templates show 300–612 remixes (ERIC COLE 612, Hanzo 486, Das Studio 473, Porto 306).
- **$29–$79** — the volume/impulse band where most trending paid templates live (MaestroClass $39, Fluence AI $49, Kensei $49, Clipster $44, Desk $79). Agency buyers who don't need budget approval buy here.
- **$99** — niche-pro band (fintech, architecture, wellness): Vectura $99, Nord-Å $99.
- **$129–$149** — flagship/anchor band with a signature feature (Compose $149, Last Studio $149, Platform, AgentLab $129, Lesmana, Jet $149 — Jet's price is explicitly justified by a "customizable hero animation").

**Portfolio-shape implication:** publish one free flagship for the remix/referral funnel, a cluster of templates in the $39–$79 band for volume, and one $129+ template to anchor the perceived quality of the whole profile. Bryn Taylor also runs an "unlimited access to all my Framer templates" bundle off-marketplace to lift average order value — Framer's 0% marketplace cut doesn't apply there since it's off-platform, but neither does the referral commission.

## Verified creator-earnings citations (use only these numbers)

- Framer paid creators **$6.5M in 2025**, with **$753K in a single November** (framer.com/creators).
- Public Framer creator-story range: **$2,291–$24,080+/month**; one documented creator's cumulative total reached **$100K** (framer.com/creators).
- Cedric (Framer creator): **$4–7K/month** (segmentui.com/learn/how-much-can-framer-and-web-designers-make-in-2024).
- Webflow seller **Zoya Qib**: ~$4,800 in year one (11 templates, 138 sales total), then ~$10,750 in the following 7 months (167 sales) — a documented ramp, not a first-month result (zoyaqib.com).
- **Bryn Taylor**: $430 in the first 12 days on one $49 portfolio template (bryntaylor.co.uk/writing/selling-webflow-templates).
- **Fluence AI** (Framer, $49): listing states "500+ copies sold" — implies roughly $24.5K gross on that single template, before whichever commission split actually applies (framer.com/community/marketplace/templates/fluence/).

No figure in the research quantifies hours invested per template, so **cost-per-hour ROI is unverified** — do not compute or claim an hourly rate from these numbers.
